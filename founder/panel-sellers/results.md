## What would flip a no

- A lower commission on the first few orders, or a clear record showing these leads close often enough that the 10% pays for itself.
- Proof that the app sends paying jobs in my area that I could not get on my own, with customers who stay on the platform.
- Seeing real paying customers in Riyadh or Cairo asking for jobs I can do, before I have to upload any papers.
- If ArmorHub showed me that most of the jobs it sends are new customers I could not get on my own, and that repeat customers are blocked from bypassing the platform, I would test it on a few jobs.
- Verified customers who are clearly ready to book, with a real track record of finished jobs and no monthly cost, at a level that clearly brings in work I wouldn't get otherwise.
- Starting to quote and get paid right away, with the approval and document upload handled after my first job, or a clear per-job fee with no document step.
- Being able to quote and see the requests straight away without uploading documents or waiting for approval, with the first customers already in the inbox.
- Seeing proof from shops like mine in Riyadh or Cairo: a real count of requests and booked jobs per month in my area, and a clear view of who the customers are before I commit.
- If approval were quick and I could see a few real nearby requests with the money held safely before I had to upload anything.
- Customers I cannot reach any other way, at a commission lower than what I spend to win them myself, with no price-only bidding.
- Seeing a real monthly count of paid, completed jobs from sellers in Riyadh or Cairo who do what I do, plus a few real requests shown to me before I have to commit anything.
- Seeing real jobs from businesses like mine in Riyadh or Cairo, with the price visible and the money actually paid out to a seller on time.
