ArmorHub sends you customers who are ready to book. They describe a job (for example paint protection film for the front of a BMW M4), nearby verified partners see the request and send a price, and the customer picks one.
Customers pay inside the app and the money is held until you finish the job; ArmorHub keeps a commission of 10% of each paid order (shared between the platform, the market company and the partner) and the rest settles to you. A written warranty is issued automatically.
Joining is free. You upload your ID and commercial registration, a reviewer checks them, and you can start sending quotes once approved. You get a request inbox, a job schedule, team accounts for your technicians and an earnings page.
Sellers of physical products can also list them on a branded store and ship with tracking; you pay no monthly fee.
The first markets are Saudi Arabia and Egypt, in Arabic and English, starting with Riyadh and Cairo.
