## What would flip a no

- A friend or family member who I trust has used ArmorHub for the same job and their car came out well.
- If my own workshop charged clearly more than the app quotes for the same film, and I could see a named partner in my city with a track record of work I could check myself.
- A partner I can visit in Riyadh before paying, or a recommendation from a workshop I already trust, plus a quick same-day price.
- A friend or family member I trust tells me they used ArmorHub and the installer did good work and honoured the warranty.
- A verified partner near me with open time slots I can pick in the app, and a warranty I can actually claim on if something goes wrong.
- Seeing verified partners with real reviews from people in my own city, and a named warranty provider I can call directly if the film fails.
- Seeing the named installer's own Google rating, reviews and photos of finished BMWs in Riyadh or Cairo, plus the written warranty terms before I pay.
- Seeing real reviews from owners of cars like mine, or my own workshop joining the platform so I can see the same people and work I already trust.
- Reviews from several owners of cars like mine, and a named company that stands behind the warranty and the money held in escrow.
- A named film brand with its own warranty in writing, and photos of finished work on M4s or similar cars from the specific partner who would do my car.
- A real review from a car owner I know, or my own workshop listed on the app.
- A friend or family member who has already used a specific ArmorHub partner in Riyadh or Cairo and tells me the work was good and the complaint process actually worked.
