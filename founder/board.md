# Board verdict: ArmorHub

**Vote: 2 fund if, 1 pass. Average score 3.3 out of 10** (Offers 3 fund if, Product 4 fund if, Monopoly 3 pass).

The board scored the business as it stands today: early, demo data only, a simulated payment gateway, and a thin per-order margin. The evidence behind it is two simulated panels (30 buyers, 20 partners). These show objections, not measured demand.

## What more than one member raised (go first)

1. **No proof yet, so no trust.** 0 of 30 simulated buyers would buy; 27 said trust (no reviews, unknown partners, an existing workshop and a WhatsApp quote). All three members flagged that the core promise (held payment, split on completion, warranty) has never run for a real person.
2. **Too broad.** Film, coating, tint, home services, beauty, devices and physical-products shipping, in two countries, all at once. All three asked for one category in one city first.
3. **Bypass.** The platform keeps about 3.5% of an order and partners already fear customers taking their number. The board wants a stated reason for both sides to keep the job on the platform after the first quote.
4. **Economics.** About SAR 4.65 contribution per order if the platform pays the gateway fee, break-even near 61 paid orders a day. Two members linked this to being unable to fund a guarantee or incentives.
5. **Distribution unsolved.** No named, costed route to the first 100 customers.
6. **Partner onboarding friction.** Document upload and an approval wait before a partner can even see a request.

## Conditions (checklist)

- [ ] One category, one city (for example paint protection film in Riyadh); pause the rest. *(a decision for the founder)*
- [ ] A live payment provider and a set number of real, paid, completed jobs with real partners. *(needs the provider; the number is the founder's)*
- [ ] Real reviews and warranties on public partner and product pages before spending on acquisition. *(reviews now exist in the product)*
- [ ] A platform-backed guarantee that fits the margin, or a changed take rate or fee handling. *(`/founder-pricing`, `/founder-cfo`)*
- [ ] An anti-bypass reason to stay on the platform: warranty and held payment only when paid in the app. *(copy and product; partly done in this round)*
- [ ] A one-sentence product and the in-app moment that keeps the job on the platform.
- [ ] A shorter partner onboarding path, or showing real requests before documents. *(partly done in this round)*
- [ ] A repeatable way to reach the first 100 buyers with a measured cost per order. *(`/founder-marketing`)*

## Where they disagree

The Monopoly lens passes on the idea as briefed (no small market owned, no moat against bypass). The Offers and Product lenses would fund it if it narrows and proves one real job end to end. That split is the useful part: the problem is focus and proof, not the technology.

## The strongest version of this business

A single-city, single-category trust layer for installing paint protection film and ceramic coating in Riyadh: verified installers, quotes in one thread, payment held until the job is done, and a warranty that is valid only when the job is paid in the app, with real reviews on every partner page. The same platform then opens the other categories and Egypt once that one loop shows repeat jobs. The board sees this as narrower than what was pitched.
