# ArmorHub (board brief)

- What it is: a multi-market marketplace where customers describe a job (car paint protection film, ceramic coating, window tint, home services, beauty, devices), nearby verified partners send quotes, the customer accepts one and pays in the app, the job is done and a warranty is issued. Sellers can also list physical products that ship with tracking from a branded store or from a market warehouse.
- Who it is for: customers in Saudi Arabia and Egypt who need a service or product and do not know whom to trust; and service partners, installers and sellers who want a steady flow of customers.
- What it sells, at what price: free for customers and partners to join. The platform takes a commission of 10% of each paid order, split 35% platform, 25% market company, 40% partner, so the platform's own take is about 3.5% of an order. Example order: paint protection film for a BMW M4 front bumper and hood, SAR 1,300 to 2,100.
- Where and how: web app in English and Arabic (right to left). Country markets Saudi Arabia and Egypt first, each a data-driven market pack (currency, VAT, phone rules). Partners are verified with ID and commercial registration documents reviewed by staff. Payment is held in the app and split on completion. Warranties are issued automatically. Public storefront per business, admin panel with live payment-provider switching, audit log, role-based access.
- Stage: early. Demo data only, a simulated payment gateway only (no live provider), no mobile app, product reviews just added, no multi-product cart, first partners being added in Riyadh and Cairo.
- Unit economics (an estimate by the founder's tooling): average order SAR 900, estimated gateway fee about SAR 25.75 per order, platform revenue per order about SAR 31.50, so about SAR 4.65 contribution per order if the platform pays the fee; break-even about 61 paid orders a day on SAR 8,500 monthly fixed costs; year one about -SAR 47,600 on operations on those assumptions.
- What 30 simulated buyers said: 0 of 30 would buy now; 27 gave trust as the reason (no reviews, unknown partners, they already have a workshop and WhatsApp quotes). What 20 simulated partners said: 8 of 20 would join (mostly hungry for customers), the rest object to the 10% commission, the document upload and approval wait, and fear of bypass.
- Constraints: small team, early stage, budget not stated.
