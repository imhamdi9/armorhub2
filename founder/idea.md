# ArmorHub

- What it is: a multi-market marketplace where customers describe a job (car protection film, ceramic coating, tint, home services, beauty, devices), nearby verified partners send quotes, the customer accepts one and pays in the app, the job is done and a warranty is issued. Sellers can also list physical products that ship with tracking.
- Who it is for: customers in Saudi Arabia and Egypt who need a service or product and do not know whom to trust; and service partners, installers and sellers who want a steady flow of customers.
- What it sells, at what price: the platform is free to customers. It keeps a commission of 10% of each paid order (split between the platform, the market company and the partner). Example: paint protection film for a BMW M4 front bumper and hood, about SAR 1,300 to 2,100 depending on the quote.
- Where and how: web app in English and Arabic (right to left), country markets Saudi Arabia and Egypt first. Partners are verified with ID and commercial registration documents. Payment is held in the app and split on completion; warranties are issued automatically.
- Budget and constraints: early stage, small team, a simulated payment gateway only so far (no live payment provider yet), no mobile app, no customer reviews until recently, no cart for multiple products, demo data only.
