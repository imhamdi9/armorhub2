ArmorHub is a website and app where you describe what you need (for example paint protection film for the front bumper and hood of your BMW M4) and nearby verified partners send you their price.
You compare the quotes, accept the one you like and pay inside the app. The money is held until the work is done and a written warranty is kept in your account.
It costs you nothing to use. Prices are set by the partners: for that job, quotes are usually around SAR 1,300 to SAR 2,100. You can cancel for free before you pay.
Partners are checked with their ID and commercial registration before they can quote. You can use it in Arabic or English, at armorhub.example.
Today the first partners are being added in Riyadh and Cairo.
