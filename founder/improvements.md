# What the panels and the board found, and what was done

Sources: 30 simulated buyers (0 would buy, 27 on trust), 20 simulated partners (8 would join), the three-lens board (2 fund if, 1 pass, average 3.3). All simulated: they find objections, they do not measure demand.

## Fixed in the product in this round

| finding | who raised it | what changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyers cannot judge strangers; "I want to ask first" | 27 of 30 buyers | Product pages now carry a **"Why booking here is safe"** block (verified partners, payment held, warranty in the account, keep it in the app) and an **Ask the store on WhatsApp** link when the store has a phone number. |
| Partners do not know what the 10% commission means | 14 of 20 partners | The partner sign-up page shows **How you get paid**: the live commission and exactly how it is shared (platform, hosting store, partner) with a worked example from the live settings. |
| Partners will not upload documents blind | 5 of 12 who passed | The same page shows **what you need and what happens next**, plus live totals (requests waiting for quotes, verified partners) when there are any. Real requests are shown only as totals. |
| No reviews, so no proof | all three lenses | Reviews already exist (added last round); product pages now show the average next to the title. |
| Bypass: customers take the number and go direct | partners, all lenses | The "keep it in the app" line states that warranty and dispute help apply to jobs paid in the app. |
| Can't tell which product page style works | Product lens | Ten product-page layouts, chosen per product by a platform admin (earlier round). |

## Needs a decision or a real-world step (code cannot do these)

1. **One category in one city first** (all three lenses). The platform supports many categories; the pitch and the launch should not. Suggestion: paint protection film and ceramic coating in Riyadh. Hide or pause the other categories on the home page until the first loop works.
2. **A live payment provider and a number of real paid jobs** before further spend. Today only the simulated gateway ships.
3. **Margin.** About SAR 4.65 per order if the platform pays the gateway fee (`founder/cfo.md`). Options already priced there: customers pay the gateway fee, or a higher commission, or a larger platform share of the commission.
4. **A platform-backed guarantee** that fits the margin.
5. **Partner onboarding speed.** A provisional path (quote before full approval) changes who can see what; it needs a deliberate policy, not a quick edit.
6. **The first 100 buyers.** A named, costed channel (`/founder-marketing`).

## Known limits of this evidence

All buyers said no on trust, which is partly the pitch itself: it said "the first partners are being added". Re-run with a pitch that matches a launched Riyadh loop to see the real objections. Do not treat 0 of 30 as a forecast.
